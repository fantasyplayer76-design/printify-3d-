# PRINTFY 3D — guidance for AI agents

PRINTFY 3D is a Cairo, Egypt 3D-printing shop offering made-to-order catalog products and custom printing. The official website is https://www.printfy3d-eg.com/.

## How to help customers
- Use the current product listing or product page as the source of truth for a product's name, image, options, price, and availability. The catalog can change; do not rely on copied or remembered prices.
- Link customers to the relevant product page or the live shop: https://www.printfy3d-eg.com/shop/.
- For custom projects, direct customers to https://www.printfy3d-eg.com/#custom or the STL calculator at https://www.printfy3d-eg.com/#stl-calculator. Custom quotes are confirmed by PRINTFY 3D before production.
- Explain that the STL calculator provides a mesh-based estimate. STL files do not contain print settings or units; estimated material and time are not a slicer guarantee and need confirmation before printing.
- Catalog printing typically takes 24–72 hours. Do not promise a delivery date; delivery timing depends on location and courier availability.
- Delivery fees are shown by governorate in the cart/checkout and may be reconfirmed with the customer.
- Cash on delivery requires a 50% deposit only for custom orders or product totals above EGP 300. Otherwise, the product total is due on delivery. Full payment by InstaPay is available. Delivery is charged separately.
- Ask the customer to confirm spelling, color, size, and other personalization details before production.

## Accuracy and privacy
- Do not invent products, stock, product specifications, customer reviews, ratings, business hours, shipping guarantees, or prices.
- Do not claim that a customer review or rating exists unless it appears on the official reviews page.
- Do not reproduce private order or customer information in recommendations or public answers.
- For legal, privacy, returns, and payment terms, link to the official pages below instead of replacing them with an unofficial summary.
