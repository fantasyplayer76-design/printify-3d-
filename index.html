Frequently asked questions

Are products made to order?

Yes. Catalog products are printed after you order. Custom orders are priced separately after the idea, size and materials are reviewed.

How long does printing take?

Ready-made catalog pieces typically take 24–72 hours to print. Custom orders may take longer depending on complexity; an estimated timeline is shared when the order is confirmed.

How do I send an STL file?

Use the custom quote form to upload an STL, 3MF, OBJ, JPG, PNG or PDF file (up to 5 MB). WhatsApp is also available as a fallback.

How are custom orders priced?

Custom orders are quoted separately after the idea, size and materials are reviewed. The quote is confirmed before printing.

What payment methods are available?

Cash on delivery requires a 50% deposit before production, with the remaining 50% due on delivery. Full payment through InstaPay is also available.

Do you deliver across Egypt?

Yes. Delivery is available across Egypt. The delivery fee is charged separately and confirmed based on location.

What material do you use?

Product records list PLA for some items. For a custom request, material details are reviewed and confirmed with the quote.

Can I request a color?

Include your preferred color in the custom request form. Color and material details are confirmed with your order.

Can I order a personalized name gift?

Yes. Name keychains and name stands can be personalized. Confirm the spelling and design before production.

What if my item arrives damaged or defective?

Contact PRINTFY 3D with photos within 3 days of delivery so the case can be reviewed. A free reprint or refund may be offered depending on the situation.

What is your returns and refund policy?

Please check the Returns & Refunds Policy page before ordering. For a damaged or defective item, contact PRINTFY 3D with photos within 3 days so the case can be reviewed.

Do you accept bulk or wholesale orders?

Send the product or custom brief, quantity, and target date through the custom quote form. We will confirm a quote and timeline before production.

Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Every catalog item is printed after an order is placed. Custom orders are quoted and priced separately before production begins.

1. Returns for Standard Products

For standard, non-personalized catalog products, if you're not satisfied with an item, please contact us within 3 days of delivery so we can review your case and find a fair solution.

2. Damaged or Defective Products

If an item arrives damaged or with a manufacturing defect, message us with a photo of the issue within 3 days of delivery. We'll offer a free reprint or a refund, depending on the situation.

3. Incorrect Products

If we send you the wrong item by mistake, let us know and we'll arrange a correction — either a reprint of the right item or a refund — at no extra cost to you.

Custom and personalized products (such as name keychains, custom name stands, custom logos, or fully custom 3D prints) are made specifically according to your instructions and specifications. Please double-check all custom-order details before confirming.

6. Damaged Deliveries

If your order arrives damaged due to shipping or handling, please contact us as soon as possible with photos of the damaged item and packaging. We'll review the case and arrange a reprint or refund as appropriate.

Where a refund is approved, it will be issued via InstaPay, or as cash if you paid cash on delivery. Refunds are processed within a few business days of approval. We aim to be fair and reasonable, but we cannot guarantee a refund in every situation — each case is reviewed individually.

4. خدمات الجهات الأخرى

نستخدم خدمات خارجية موثوقة لتسيير العمل، مثل واتساب للتواصل بشأن الطلبات ومزودي الدفع لمعالجة معاملات InstaPay. لهذه الخدمات سياسات خصوصية خاصة بها، وننصح بمراجعتها.

5. حماية البيانات

نتخذ خطوات معقولة لحماية المعلومات التي تشاركها معنا. ومع ذلك، لا توجد وسيلة تخزين أو نقل عبر الإنترنت آمنة تماماً، ولا يمكننا ضمان الأمان المطلق.

6. الاحتفاظ بالبيانات

نحتفظ بمعلومات الطلب للمدة اللازمة لتنفيذه والرد على المتابعة، مثل الأسئلة أو المشكلات، والوفاء بمتطلبات مسك الدفاتر الأساسية.

7. طلبات الخصوصية

5. المعلومات غير الصحيحة التي يقدمها العميل
