    Terms of Service

    Last updated: September 2026

    1. About PRINTFY 3D

    PRINTFY 3D ("we", "us", "our") is an online 3D printing business based in Cairo, Egypt. We design, print, and deliver 3D-printed décor, figures, toys, accessories, personalized products, and custom 3D prints. By placing an order or using this website, you agree to these Terms of Service.

    2. Products and Custom Orders

    We offer both ready-made catalog products and fully custom 3D printing services. Custom orders are created based on the description, images, files, or specifications you provide to us, typically through our website or WhatsApp.

    3. Product Variations Caused by 3D Printing

    3D printing is a layer-by-layer manufacturing process, and small variations are a normal part of it. Color shade, surface texture, and exact dimensions may vary slightly from the reference photos on our website or from one print to the next. These variations are expected and do not necessarily indicate a defect.

    4. Customer Responsibility for Custom-Order Information

    For custom and personalized orders, you are responsible for providing accurate information — including names, spelling, sizes, colors, and any files or images. We print based on what you provide, so please review your order details carefully before confirming.

    5. Prices and Orders

    All prices shown on our website are listed in Egyptian Pounds (EGP) and cover the standard base product. Additional charges may apply for extra colors, gift packaging, logo conversion, new custom designs, or rush orders. Delivery is charged separately depending on your location. Prices are subject to change without prior notice, but the price confirmed at the time of your order will be honored.

    6. Production and Delivery

    Standard catalog items are typically printed within 24–72 hours. Custom orders may take longer depending on complexity, and we'll share an estimated timeline when your order is confirmed. We deliver across Egypt; delivery times may vary based on your location and courier availability.

    7. Customer-Provided Images, Logos, Designs, and Files

    If you send us images, logos, designs, or 3D model files for a custom print, you confirm that you own the rights to that content or have permission to have it reproduced. We reserve the right to decline any request that appears to infringe on someone else's intellectual property or that we consider inappropriate.

    8. Payments

    We accept cash on delivery and InstaPay. Orders are confirmed with you directly — typically via WhatsApp — regardless of the payment method chosen.

    9. Order Changes and Cancellations

    You may request changes or cancel your order before production has started, at no charge. Once printing has begun — especially for custom or personalized items — changes or cancellations may no longer be possible, since the item is being made specifically for you. See our Refund & Returns Policy for more detail.

    Refund & Returns Policy

    Last updated: September 2026

    1. Returns for Standard Products

    For standard, non-personalized catalog products, if you're not satisfied with an item, please contact us within 3 days of delivery so we can review your case and find a fair solution.

    2. Damaged or Defective Products

    If an item arrives damaged or with a manufacturing defect, message us with a photo of the issue within 3 days of delivery. We'll offer a free reprint or a refund, depending on the situation.

    3. Incorrect Products

    If we send you the wrong item by mistake, let us know and we'll arrange a correction — either a reprint of the right item or a refund — at no extra cost to you.

    4. Custom-Made Products

    Custom and personalized products (such as name keychains, custom name stands, custom logos, or fully custom 3D prints) are made specifically according to your instructions and specifications. Because of this, custom-made products generally cannot be returned or refunded once production has started, except in the cases described in Sections 2 and 3 above.

    5. Incorrect Customer Information

    If a custom order is printed exactly as you specified (for example, a name spelled the way you provided it, or a size/color you selected) but you later notice a mistake in the information you gave us, this is not considered a defect, and standard refund terms may not apply. Please double-check all custom-order details before confirming.

    6. Damaged Deliveries

    If your order arrives damaged due to shipping or handling, please contact us as soon as possible with photos of the damaged item and packaging. We'll review the case and arrange a reprint or refund as appropriate.

    7. Refunds

    Where a refund is approved, it will be issued via InstaPay, or as cash if you paid cash on delivery. Refunds are processed within a few business days of approval. We aim to be fair and reasonable, but we cannot guarantee a refund in every situation — each case is reviewed individually.

    8. Order Cancellation

    You can cancel an order free of charge before production has started. Once printing has begun, cancellation may not be possible, particularly for custom or personalized items.
