How much does 3D printing cost in Egypt?

There isn’t one fixed price for every print. The final price depends on the model and how it needs to be made. Here are the main things that affect a quote.

What affects the price?

  • Size and material: larger pieces use more filament. Material choice and color availability can also affect the quote.
  • Print time and detail: intricate shapes, fine layers and supports can take longer to print.
  • Finishing: assembly, cleanup, painting or other finishing work adds time.
  • Quantity: multiple copies may be planned together, though each piece still uses material and printer time.
  • Personalization: names, logos and design changes can require preparation before printing.

How to get a useful custom quote

Send us the STL or 3D file if you have one. If not, send a photo, sketch or example, with the approximate size, quantity, preferred color and delivery city. We’ll review the details and confirm the price in EGP before production starts.

Why can two prints of the same size cost differently?

Two objects can use different amounts of material and printer time. A solid, detailed model may take longer than a simple hollow shape. Finishing and personalization also change the work involved, so dimensions alone don’t determine the price.
